  - heading: "The brief"
    body: |-
      Reposition WAMA with a brand that better reflects its maturity, values, and evolving role in the WA marketing community, four years on from its establishment.
  - heading: "The scope"
    body: |-
      A full brand strategy refresh including purpose, vision, brand pillars, audience segmentation, and tone of voice.
  - heading: "The process"
    body: |-
      I started by identifying what still resonated with WAMA — their pride in WA-born marketing and commitment to DEI.

      From this, I:

      - Defined a clear and unified purpose and vision.

      - Developed four brand pillars to articulate WAMA’s role in the community.

      - Identified primary and secondary audiences to guide communications and event planning.

      - Created a set of tone of voice principles to ensure consistency across all touchpoints—balancing playfulness with confidence.
  - heading: "The outcome"
    body: |-
      The rebrand gave WAMA a renewed sense of identity and direction. It provided clarity for internal decision-making and external communications, and laid the groundwork for more cohesive, confident engagement with their community.
