  - heading: "The brief"
    body: |-
      Develop a distinctive brand and comprehensive comms strategy for Australia’s first crowd equity funded solar farm, Grong Grong Solar Farm —positioning it as a pioneering force in renewable energy and attracting investor interest.
  - heading: "The scope"
    body: |-
      The project included brand development, tone of voice principles, audience education, and a full suite of launch comms across digital and print channels.
  - heading: "The process"
    body: |-
      I led the creation of Grong Grong’s brand identity and messaging framework, with a focus on clarity, trust, and accessibility for potential investors. Key contributions included:

      Developed a succinct and future-focused mission and vision that clearly articulated Grong Grong’s role in Australia’s renewable energy landscape.

      Created tone of voice principles that positioned the brand as accessible, trustworthy, and knowledgeable.

      Conducted a competitor analysis to identify Grong Grong’s unique value proposition and opportunity to set the industry standard.

      Delivered an extensive communications plan, including:

      A new website

      An offer document for investors

      A series of press releases for key industry publications

      EDMs and a webinar series to educate and engage the audience
  - heading: "The outcome"
    body: |-
      Grong Grong launched with a strong, differentiated brand and a clear message that resonated with its target audience. The communications strategy helped establish credibility, drive awareness, and support investor engagement.
